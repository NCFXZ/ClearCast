Actual fullscreen Chinese and English captures: waiting, music, lyrics and settings in light/dark themes, plus video with controls and immersive video (10 per locale, 20 total).

Original demonstration content only; no private device content or third-party album art. Dimensions, PNG/RGB hashes and edge measurements are in [provenance.json](provenance.json).
