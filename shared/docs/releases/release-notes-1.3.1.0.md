# ClearCast 1.3.1.0

- 音乐和视频投屏默认自动全屏。
- 新增自动显示音乐歌词开关，默认开启。
- 全屏控制条采用悬浮磨砂样式，与窗口模式保持相同宽度。
- 修复异常曲名或歌词导致的卡顿，以及暂停视频退出全屏后背景不更新的问题。

## English

- Music and video sessions now enter full screen by default.
- Adds an enabled-by-default setting to show music lyrics automatically.
- Full-screen controls now float with a frosted background and match their windowed width.
- Fixes slowdowns with malformed track titles or lyrics, and a stale background when leaving full screen with video paused.
