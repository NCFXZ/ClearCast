# ClearCast 1.3.2.0

- 将视频控制栏的模糊计算移到后台；持续过载时自动降低辅助背景采样频率，恢复后逐步回到原档，正常性能下保留现有效果。
- 改进停止接收时的任务取消和迟到结果处理，减少封面、歌词和背景工作积压。
- 统一播放图标、控制栏间距和主题色进度条，改善歌词开关过渡与触控板滚动体验。
- 页面保留默认切换动画，视频独立显示；首页动图使用 30 FPS。

## English

- Moves video control blur calculations to the background. Sustained overload reduces auxiliary background sampling, with gradual recovery and unchanged effects under normal load.
- Improves cancellation and stale-result handling when stopping reception to keep artwork, lyrics, and background work bounded.
- Aligns playback icons and control spacing, unifies themed progress bars, and improves lyric transitions and trackpad scrolling.
- Keeps the default page transitions while displaying video independently; the home animation runs at 30 FPS.
